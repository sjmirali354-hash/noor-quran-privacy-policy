Privacy Policy

Noor Quran · com.sj64noorquran

🛡 Our Commitment to You

Noor Quran was created as a free Islamic application for the Muslim community. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

We do not require account registration, and Noor Quran itself does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information. This Privacy Policy explains how the application works, what information may be processed, and how third-party services are used.

📖 Information We Collect

Noor Quran does not ask you to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The application stores your personal preferences locally on your device, including your selected language, bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh count, notification settings, downloaded content, and appearance preferences.

Some third-party services used by Noor Quran, such as advertising or content providers, may automatically receive limited technical information (for example IP address, device information, or advertising identifiers) according to their own Privacy Policies.

📍 Location Permission

Noor Quran may request access to your device's location to automatically detect your city and calculate accurate Prayer Times and the correct Qibla direction.

Your location is used only to provide these Islamic features. Noor Quran does not create a personal location history or require you to create an account.

If you prefer, you can manually enter your city without granting location permission.

🔔 Notification Permission

If you enable Prayer reminders, Azan notifications, or Dhikr reminders, Noor Quran will request notification permission from your device.

These reminders are intended to run locally on your device. Their delivery may also depend on your operating system, battery optimization settings, and notification permissions.

You may disable notifications at any time from your Android device settings.

🎧 Quran Audio Streaming

Quran recitations may be streamed or downloaded from cdn.islamic.network or other trusted Islamic content providers used by Noor Quran.

Whenever you play or download audio, your device sends a normal internet request to the provider in order to deliver the requested audio file.

Downloaded recitations are stored locally on your device for offline listening. Removing app data or uninstalling the application may delete downloaded files.

📢 Advertising — Google AdMob

To support the continued development of Noor Quran, the Android version of the application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its partners may process technical information such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device information, app interactions, and approximate location according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Depending on your country and privacy settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage your advertising preferences through your Android or Google privacy settings at any time.

💾 Local Storage & Offline Data

Noor Quran stores your bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh counter, downloaded Surahs, selected language, notification preferences, appearance settings, and other app preferences locally on your device.

This information is stored using your device's local storage technologies, such as localStorage, IndexedDB, or application storage, depending on your platform.

Clearing application data, clearing browser storage, uninstalling the application, or resetting your device may permanently remove this locally stored information.

🌐 Third-Party APIs & Services

Noor Quran uses trusted third-party services to provide Quran text, translations, prayer times, Qibla calculations, audio streaming, and advertisements.

These services may include:

• AlQuran Cloud API — Quran text and translations.

• Aladhan Prayer Times API — Prayer Times and Qibla calculations.

• Islamic Network CDN — Quran audio streaming.

• Google AdMob — Advertising in the Android application.

🔒 Data Sharing & Security

Noor Quran does not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may only be processed by the trusted service providers described in this Privacy Policy when necessary to provide app functionality, display advertisements, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the application.

Although we use reasonable security practices, no application or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

👶 Children's Privacy

Noor Quran is intended for a general audience and contains Islamic educational content suitable for all ages.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of internet-connected features where appropriate.
