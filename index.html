Privacy Policy

Noor Quran · com.sj64noorquran
♢
♢ Our Commitment to You

Noor Quran was built as a free service to the Muslim community. We take your privacy seriously — we do not collect personal data, we do not track you, and we do not sell your information. This policy explains exactly what we do and do not do.

وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ
“Allah knows what you conceal and what you reveal.” — Quran 16:19
▣Information We Collect

Noor Quran does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. The only data stored is your in-app preferences — chosen language, city, dark-mode setting, bookmarks, and favorites — and these are stored locally on your device only. No personal data is ever transmitted to our servers.

⌖Location Permission

The app may request access to your device's GPS location to automatically detect your city and calculate accurate Prayer Times and Qibla direction. Your precise coordinates are used solely for this calculation and are never uploaded, stored on a remote server, or shared with third parties. You can also enter your city manually at any time without granting location permission.

♢Notification Permission

If you enable Islamic reminders (Dhikr, prayer alerts), the app requests notification permission from your device. All notifications are scheduled locally — no push-notification service or external server is involved. Turning off notifications in your device settings will stop all reminders immediately.

◖Quran Audio Streaming

Audio recitations are streamed on-demand from cdn.islamic.network, a trusted public Islamic CDN. When you play or download an ayah, a standard HTTP request is sent to fetch the audio file. No account, login, or personal identifier is attached to these requests. Downloaded audio is saved locally in your browser's IndexedDB for offline use and never shared.

$Advertising — Google AdMob

To support the free development of Noor Quran, ads provided by Google AdMob may be displayed when the app runs as a native Android application. Google may use your device's advertising ID (GAID) for ad personalisation in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your Android device settings under Google → Ads. The web version of Noor Quran does not serve AdMob ads.

▤Local Storage & Offline Data

Bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh counts, notification preferences, downloaded surah text, and language settings are stored in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB. This data remains entirely on your device. Clearing your browser/app data will erase these preferences.

⌁Third-Party API Usage

Noor Quran fetches Quran text and translations from api.alquran.cloud and prayer times from api.aladhan.com. These are free public Islamic APIs. Requests include only query parameters (surah number, city name, etc.) — no personal identifiers are appended.

▢Data Security & Sharing

Noor Quran does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party beyond the service providers described above. We have no analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, or tracking library embedded in the app. Your usage of this app remains private.

Children's Privacy

Noor Quran does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and contains Islamic educational content suitable for all ages. No age-gated features or account creation is required.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of Noor Quran after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
