Privacy Policy

Noor Quran · com.sj64noorquran

Our Commitment to You

Noor Quran was built as a free service to the Muslim community. We take your privacy seriously — we do not collect personal data, we do not track you, and we do not sell your information.

This policy explains exactly what we do and do not do.

وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ

"Allah knows what you conceal and what you reveal." — Quran 16:19

Information We Collect

Noor Quran does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The only data stored is your in-app preferences including:

  • Chosen language
  • City selection
  • Dark mode setting
  • Bookmarks and favorites

These are stored locally on your device only.

Location Permission

The app may request access to your device's GPS location to calculate accurate Prayer Times and Qibla direction.

Your location is never uploaded, stored remotely, or shared with third parties.

Notification Permission

If you enable Islamic reminders or prayer alerts, the app may request notification permission from your device.

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Quran Audio Streaming

Audio recitations are streamed from trusted Islamic CDNs when requested by the user.

No account or personal identifier is attached to these requests.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Noor Quran may display ads provided by Google AdMob.

Google may use your device advertising ID (GAID) according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Local Storage & Offline Data

Bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh counts, notification settings, and downloaded content may be stored locally on your device for offline usage.

Third-Party API Usage

Noor Quran may use trusted third-party Islamic services for Quran content, prayer times, and advertisements.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • AlQuran Cloud API
  • Aladhan Prayer Times API
  • Google AdMob
  • Islamic Network CDN

Children's Privacy

Noor Quran does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties beyond trusted services required for app functionality.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
