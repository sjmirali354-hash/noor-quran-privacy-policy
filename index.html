Privacy Policy

Noor Quran · com.sj64noorquran

Our Commitment to You

Noor Quran was built as a free service for the Muslim community. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

We do not collect personal data, track users, or sell information.

وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تُسِرُّونَ وَمَا تُعْلِنُونَ

"Allah knows what you conceal and what you reveal." — Quran 16:19

Information We Collect

Noor Quran does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Passwords

The app may store user preferences locally on the device including:

  • Language settings
  • Bookmarks and favorites
  • Dark mode settings
  • Prayer reminder preferences

This data remains on your device only.

Location Permission

The app may request location permission to calculate accurate Prayer Times and Qibla direction.

Location information is never stored remotely or shared with third parties.

Notification Permission

The app may request notification permission for prayer alerts and Islamic reminders.

All notifications are generated locally on the device.

Internet Access

Noor Quran uses internet access to:

  • Load Quran text and translations
  • Stream Quran audio recitations
  • Display Islamic content
  • Load advertisements through Google AdMob

Advertising — Google AdMob

Noor Quran may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google may use device identifiers according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

  • AlQuran Cloud API
  • Aladhan Prayer Times API
  • Google AdMob
  • Islamic Network CDN

Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Policy

Children's Privacy

Noor Quran does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Data Security

We do not sell, trade, or share personal user information with third parties except trusted services required for app functionality.
