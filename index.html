Privacy Policy

Noor Quran · com.sj64noorquran

🛡 Our Commitment to You

Noor Quran was created as a free Islamic application for the Muslim community. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

We do not require account registration, and Noor Quran itself does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, password, or other personally identifiable information.

If you choose to use AI Teacher, limited device-linked learning records and encrypted recovery data may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and Noor Quran does not intentionally create personal advertising profiles from your Islamic learning activity.

📖 Information We Collect

Noor Quran does not ask you to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Core app preferences are generally stored locally on your device. These may include your selected language, city, bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh count, notification settings, downloaded content, dark-mode or appearance preferences, and other app settings.

If you use AI Teacher, the application creates a device-associated Teacher identity so that your learning progress can be maintained without requiring an email address, password, or traditional login account.

AI Teacher may process learning information such as lesson completion status, pronunciation scores, practice scores, lesson history, retry information, revision information, timestamps, and related learning preferences.

An encrypted recovery snapshot containing relevant Teacher progress may be synchronized with Noor Quran's server to support recovery after reinstalling the application.

Some third-party services used by Noor Quran, including advertising and content providers, may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, device information, advertising identifiers, or other information according to their own Privacy Policies.

🌐 Automatic Translation Language Selection

During the first launch, Noor Quran may automatically suggest a default Quran translation language based on your device language, region, or country.

You can change your preferred Quran translation language at any time from the application's Settings.

Once selected, your preferred translation language is remembered and always takes priority over automatic language detection.

📍 Location Permission

Noor Quran may request access to your device's location to automatically detect your city and calculate accurate Prayer Times and the correct Qibla direction.

When location permission is enabled, your precise GPS coordinates may be used on your device for these calculations.

Your precise location is not used to create a personal location history and is not uploaded to or stored on Noor Quran's remote server for these features.

Noor Quran does not require an account to use location-based Prayer Times or Qibla features.

If you prefer, you can manually enter your city without granting location permission.

🔔 Notification Permission

If you enable Prayer reminders, Azan notifications, Dhikr reminders, or other Islamic reminders, Noor Quran will request notification permission from your device.

These reminders are intended to be scheduled and delivered locally on your device. Noor Quran does not require a remote push-notification service or external notification server for these local reminders.

Their delivery may also depend on your operating system, battery optimization settings, notification permissions, and device configuration.

You may disable notifications at any time from your Android device settings.

🎧 Quran Audio Streaming

Quran recitations may be streamed or downloaded from cdn.islamic.network or other trusted Islamic content providers used by Noor Quran.

Whenever you play or download audio, your device sends a normal internet request to the selected provider in order to retrieve the requested audio file.

These audio requests do not require an account or login, and Noor Quran does not intentionally attach your personal profile information to the audio request.

Downloaded recitations are stored locally on your device for offline listening. Removing application data or uninstalling Noor Quran may delete downloaded audio files.

📢 Advertising — Google AdMob

To support the continued development of Noor Quran, the Android version of the application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Before certain AI Teacher lessons, the application may offer or display a full-screen rewarded advertisement. The advertisement may be unavailable, may fail to load, and is not guaranteed to appear on every device or in every region.

Google and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising information such as your Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device information, app interactions, approximate location, and related advertising information according to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable privacy settings.

Depending on your country, device settings, and applicable privacy requirements, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Noor Quran does not sell your personal information or use your AI Teacher learning records for advertising.

You can manage your advertising and personalization preferences through your Android device or Google privacy settings.

The web version of Noor Quran does not serve Google AdMob advertisements.

🎙 AI Teacher — Speech Recognition & Pronunciation Assessment

AI Teacher requests microphone access only when you choose to start a speech exercise.

Speech recognition is performed using the speech recognition service available on your device, with the Arabic Saudi Arabia (ar-SA) language setting.

Depending on your device and operating system, spoken responses may be processed by the device's speech recognition service to produce a temporary recognition result.

Noor Quran does not record, permanently store, or upload raw microphone audio.

The temporary recognized transcript is compared with the lesson target to generate an approximate pronunciation score, pass or retry result, and automated learning feedback.

The temporary recognition result is discarded from the active recognition flow after the assessment.

AI Teacher pronunciation results are intended to provide approximate educational guidance and should not be considered a formal religious certification, scholarly ruling, or professional pronunciation assessment.

👨‍🏫 AI Teacher — Progress & Feedback

If you use AI Teacher, Noor Quran may save your lesson completion status, pronunciation scores, practice scores, timestamps, retry and lesson history, revision information, and related learning preferences.

These records help provide features such as daily lesson limits, completed-lesson practice, Smart Revision, progress displays, and recovery of learning progress after reinstalling the application.

The records are associated with a device-linked Teacher identity rather than an email/password login account.

An encrypted recovery snapshot containing relevant learning information may be synchronized with Noor Quran's server to support recovery after reinstalling the application.

AI Teacher feedback is automatically generated learning guidance. It should not be treated as a formal religious certification or a substitute for qualified Islamic scholarship.

🗂 AI Teacher Data Retention & Recovery

Local AI Teacher information remains on your device until the application data is cleared, the relevant local storage is removed, or the application is uninstalled.

If you use AI Teacher, the encrypted recovery snapshot and associated device-linked learning progress may remain on Noor Quran's server to support recovery after reinstalling the application.

Noor Quran does not require an email account or password for AI Teacher.

The device-linked Teacher records are maintained only to provide the AI Teacher learning, progress, recovery, and related application features described in this Privacy Policy.

Noor Quran does not sell these learning records or use them for advertising purposes.

💾 Local Storage & Offline Data

Noor Quran stores bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh counts, downloaded Surahs, selected language, notification preferences, appearance settings, AI Teacher local learning information, and other app preferences locally on your device.

Depending on the platform, this information may be stored using technologies such as localStorage, IndexedDB, or application storage.

Local data remains on your device unless it is removed by clearing application data, clearing browser storage, uninstalling the application, or resetting your device.

Downloaded Quran content and audio intended for offline use are also stored locally and are not automatically uploaded to a personal online account.

🔐 Permissions & Your Choices

The permissions requested by Noor Quran are optional and are used only for the corresponding application features.

Location: Used for automatic city detection, Prayer Times, and Qibla. You can manually enter your city instead.

Microphone: Used only when you choose to start an AI Teacher speech exercise.

Notifications: Used for Azan, Prayer Times, Dhikr, and other Islamic reminders.

You can grant, deny, or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's application settings.

Denying or revoking a permission may disable the related feature, while the rest of Noor Quran can continue to function.

🌐 Third-Party APIs & Services

Noor Quran uses trusted third-party services to provide Quran text, translations, Prayer Times, Qibla calculations, Quran audio streaming, and advertisements.

These services may include:

• AlQuran Cloud API — Quran text and translations.

• Aladhan Prayer Times API — Prayer Times and Qibla calculations.

• Islamic Network CDN — Quran audio streaming and related Islamic content delivery.

• Google AdMob — Advertising in the Android application.

These third-party providers may process technical information required to respond to requests or provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

📡 Quran, Translation & Prayer API Requests

Noor Quran may request Quran text, translations, Prayer Times, and related Islamic information from public third-party APIs.

For example, requests to Quran and Prayer APIs may contain technical query information such as a Surah number, translation identifier, city name, country, calculation method, or other parameters required to provide the requested result.

Noor Quran does not intentionally append your name, email address, phone number, password, or personal account information to these API requests.

Third-party services may nevertheless receive standard technical information associated with an internet request, such as an IP address, according to their own infrastructure and privacy policies.

🎧 Audio Provider Requests

When you stream or download a Quran recitation, your device communicates with the selected audio provider to retrieve the requested audio file.

A standard HTTP or HTTPS request may contain normal technical information required for internet communication, such as your IP address and connection information.

Noor Quran does not intentionally include your personal profile, email address, password, or account login information in these audio requests.

Downloaded audio intended for offline listening is stored locally on your device.

📊 No Analytics, Tracking or Crash-Reporting SDK

Noor Quran does not intentionally include an analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, or tracking library designed to monitor individual users' activity.

The application does not intentionally create personal usage profiles for analytics purposes and does not sell user activity information to advertisers.

This does not prevent third-party services that are necessary for specific features, such as Google AdMob, from independently processing technical, advertising, security, or measurement information according to their own policies.

🔒 Data Sharing & Security

Noor Quran does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by the trusted service providers described in this Privacy Policy when necessary to provide application functionality, deliver Quran or Prayer content, provide audio, display advertisements, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the application and its users.

If you use AI Teacher, limited device-linked learning records and encrypted recovery data may be processed by Noor Quran's server solely to provide Teacher progress, learning, and recovery functionality.

Noor Quran does not use AI Teacher learning records for advertising or sell those records to third parties.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the application and its services.

However, no application, device, server, or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

🗑 Data Retention & Deletion

Local preferences and offline content remain on your device until you clear the relevant application or browser data, remove the stored information, uninstall Noor Quran, or reset your device.

If you use AI Teacher, the encrypted recovery snapshot and associated device-linked learning progress may remain on Noor Quran's server to support recovery after reinstalling the application.

Noor Quran does not use email accounts, passwords, or traditional user-created login accounts for this recovery system.

Removing local application data does not necessarily remove an existing server-side encrypted AI Teacher recovery record immediately, because the server record may be retained to support reinstall and recovery functionality.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Noor Quran is intended for a general audience and contains Islamic educational content suitable for all ages.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

No age-gated account registration is required to use the core application.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of internet-connected features where appropriate, including third-party services and advertising.

⚙️ App Permissions & Device Controls

You remain in control of the permissions granted to Noor Quran through your device's operating system.

You can review, grant, deny, or revoke available permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

Revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but it does not prevent you from using unrelated parts of Noor Quran.
