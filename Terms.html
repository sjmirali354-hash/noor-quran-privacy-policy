Noor Quran's AI Teacher provides educational Quran-reading practice. It may ask you to pronounce Arabic words, phrases, or passages aloud and uses speech recognition to assess the spoken response.

The AI Teacher requests microphone permission only when you choose to start a speech exercise. You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time.

Speech recognition uses the Arabic Saudi Arabia (ar-SA) recognition setting. Recognition results may be affected by accent, pronunciation, background noise, microphone quality, device settings, internet availability, operating-system services, or limitations of the speech-recognition service.

The application compares the recognized response with the lesson target and may generate an approximate pronunciation score, pass/retry result, and automated instructional feedback.

AI Teacher pronunciation scores are approximate learning aids and are not a formal Tajweed certification, religious ruling, medical assessment, or substitute for a qualified Quran teacher.

AI Teacher may provide bounded retries, listen-only guidance, completed-lesson review, practice sessions, Smart Revision, and daily new-lesson limits.

AI Teacher feedback and pronunciation results are automatically generated and may be incomplete, inconsistent, or inaccurate. When accurate Quranic recitation is important, verify your recitation with the Quran text, a trusted reciter, and a qualified Quran teacher.
